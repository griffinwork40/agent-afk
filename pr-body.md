## Summary

Splits 4 grandfathered TUI files under the 350-code-line ceiling and removes them from `.filesize-baseline.json`. Refs #832 #1865.

| File | Before | After | Sibling(s) |
|------|--------|-------|------------|
| `terminal-compositor.input-dispatch.ts` | 507 | 296 | `.enter.ts` (143), `.cursor.ts` (83) |
| `_lib/testing/virtual-screen.ts` | 422 | 298 | `.csi.ts` (149) |
| `elicitation/agent-question.ts` | 399 | 196 | `.fallback.ts` (262) |
| `commands/interactive/tool-lane.ts` | 364 | 243 | `.flush.ts` (151) |

**Concerns moved:**

- `terminal-compositor.input-dispatch.enter.ts` -- `handleEnter`, `handleBackspace`, `mergeSubmissionPayloads`, `commitIfChanged`, `applyAtomicPlaceholderDelete`. Enter is the most complex handler (259 grandfathered lines); backspace and helpers are its immediate neighbors in the guard chain.
- `terminal-compositor.input-dispatch.cursor.ts` -- `handleCursorAndEdit` (readline-parity cursor/edit bindings). Imports `commitIfChanged` and `applyAtomicPlaceholderDelete` from `.enter.ts`.
- `virtual-screen.csi.ts` -- `dispatchCsi`, `normalizeCsiParams`, and all 6 erase helpers. `VirtualScreen.dispatchCsi` becomes a one-line delegator using a structurally-compatible cast (private fields prevent `implements CsiHost` directly).
- `agent-question.fallback.ts` -- `renderConfirmFallback`, `renderChoiceFallback`, `renderMultiChoiceFallback`, `renderNumberFallback`, `renderTextFallback`. The non-overlay readLine paths for daemon/non-TTY surfaces. `SKIP` constant moved here and re-exported from `agent-question.ts` to preserve the public surface.
- `tool-lane.flush.ts` -- `flushSource` and `flushCompletedRoots`. Both methods delegate to the sibling via `as unknown as ToolLaneFlushHost` (same pattern as virtual-screen; `private` prevents structural assignment).

## Public surface

Original files keep their exact public export surfaces. No importer was rewritten. `SKIP` is re-exported from `agent-question.ts` to satisfy existing consumers.
