feat(#2944): burn-rate projection for subscription-quota indicator

Adds a burn-rate projection (`cap in ~40m at this rate`) to the 5h/7d
subscription-quota indicator and footer, accounting for rolling-window
decay so idle sessions never over-predict exhaustion.

Architecture:

* `src/agent/usage/burn-rate.ts` (new) — pure projection math: computes
  a time-to-cap ETA from a bounded ring of `{observedAt, utilization,
  resetsAt}` samples. Suppression rules:
  - Fewer than MIN_SAMPLES (3) recent samples → no projection.
  - No adjacent rising pair (idle/decaying session) → no projection.
  - Net oldest→newest delta ≤ 0 → no projection.
  - Projected cap is after resetsAt → no projection (window resets before cap).
  - Samples older than MAX_SAMPLE_AGE_MS (12 min) are dropped.

* `src/agent/usage/usage-record.ts` — adds `WindowHistorySample` type
  and an optional `history?: WindowHistorySample[]` ring to
  `WindowsObservation`. `mergeWindows` now unions history rings;
  `parseUsageRecord` parses and validates history from stored JSON.
  Backward-compatible: absent history = no projection.

* `src/agent/usage/usage-ledger.ts` — `writeMerged` enriches every
  incoming windows observation with a new history sample (binding window)
  before merging into the stored record, so the ring grows automatically
  with normal usage traffic.

* `src/cli/quota-indicator.ts` — `quotaWindowsFromRecord` (new) builds
  `QuotaWindows` from a full `UsageRecord` (with history). The render loop
  computes a burn-rate projection on the binding window and appends
  `cap ~Xm` in the meta tone when projectable. `formatQuotaIndicator`
  unchanged signature; new optional `history` field on `QuotaWindowState`.

* `src/cli/quota-footer.ts` — `burnRateClause` appends
  `· cap in ~Xm at this rate` to the caution/near/over footer lines when
  history supports it.

* `src/cli/commands/interactive/shared.ts` and `turn-handler.footer.ts`
  — prefer `quotaWindowsFromRecord(readUsageRecord(...))` so history
  flows from the ledger; fall back to `quotaWindowsFromSnapshot` when
  the ledger is empty.

Tests:

* `src/agent/usage/burn-rate.test.ts` (new, 15 tests):
  - Idle/decaying session (decreasing utilization) → no projection.
  - Stale samples → no projection.
  - Projected cap after reset → no projection.
  - Steady burn → sane ETA before resetsAt, correct ratePerMs.
  - Ring size and immutability invariants for `appendSample`.

All existing quota-indicator (24), quota-footer (18), usage-core (20),
usage-formatter (20), and codex-usage (9) tests continue to pass.
